# 🏥 Hospital Management System

A comprehensive hospital management platform designed to streamline healthcare operations through intelligent patient management, digital medical records, automated billing, and healthcare workflow optimization.

The system centralizes hospital operations into a unified platform, enabling efficient resource management, improved patient care, and data-driven decision making for healthcare professionals.

## 🚀 Features

### 👨‍⚕️ Intelligent Patient Management
- Patient registration and profile management.
- Centralized patient information system.
- Quick retrieval of patient history and treatment records.

### 🧠 Smart Medical Records System
- Digital storage of patient records.
- Intelligent organization of treatment histories.
- Faster access to critical medical information.

### 💊 Prescription & Treatment Management
- Digital prescription generation and management.
- Treatment tracking and record maintenance.
- Medication history monitoring.

### 🧪 Laboratory Information System
- Laboratory test scheduling and tracking.
- Digital report management.
- Integration of lab results with patient records.

### 🏨 Smart Room Allocation
- Automated room assignment workflows.
- Occupancy tracking and availability monitoring.
- Efficient hospital resource utilization.

### 👩‍⚕️ Staff & Workforce Management
- Staff and nurse profile management.
- Department-wise workforce organization.
- Administrative workflow optimization.

### 💳 Automated Billing System
- Medical bill generation and tracking.
- Treatment cost calculation.
- Digital payment and billing records.

### 📊 Healthcare Analytics Dashboard
- Hospital activity monitoring.
- Patient admission and discharge insights.
- Resource utilization reporting.
- Operational performance analysis.

## 🎯 Project Goal

The Hospital Management System aims to modernize healthcare administration by combining intelligent workflow automation, digital record management, and data-driven hospital operations.

By reducing manual paperwork and streamlining hospital processes, the platform improves efficiency, enhances patient care, and supports better healthcare decision-making.
